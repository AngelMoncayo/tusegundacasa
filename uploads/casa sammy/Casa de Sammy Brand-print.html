06 · Voice

Unhurried. Bilingual. Embodied.

We speak the way water moves through stone — slowly, warmly, always finding the body. Short sentences. Sensory verbs. Spanish and English side by side, never translated word-for-word.

Principle 01

Pocas palabras,
mucho silencio.

Leave space. A caption of six words beats a paragraph. White space is a design element and a feeling.

Principle 02

Invita al cuerpo.

Use verbs you can feel — respira, descansa, hunde, tibia, cerrado. Avoid corporate wellness vocabulary.

Principle 03

Bilingüe,
nunca traducido.

English and Spanish live together. Each says what it says best. We never translate line for line.

Sample copy

Cierra los ojos. Close the door behind you. Aquí no hay prisa que te alcance.

08 · Do's & Don'ts

Guardrails.

Small decisions, made often, that keep the brand whole.

Let the mark breathe.

Generous clearspace — always at least the x-height of the wordmark on all sides.

Never stretch.

No condensing, skewing, or rotating the logo. Scale proportionally.

Pair serif with sans.

Cormorant + Inter only. No substitutions. Italiana reserved for signatures.

No bright accents.

Never place the mark on saturated backgrounds — no primary red, blue, or yellow. Earth tones only.

Spanish first.

On bilingual materials, Spanish leads. English follows, in italic or lighter weight.

No emoji, no gradients.

Keep surfaces flat and typographic. Let texture come from paper, linen, and photography.
