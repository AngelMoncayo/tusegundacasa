01 · The image

An overhead table, three faces lit only by their screens.

A wooden family dining table at night, photographed from directly above. Three figures are seated around it — a teenage girl, a middle-aged man in a button-up, an elderly woman in reading glasses — each one absorbed in a phone. Their faces are lit only by the cold bluish glow of their screens.

The food on the table is warm but untouched: ceramic bowls, a half-full glass of wine, a cup of coffee gone cold. Warm amber light sits on the dishes. Cold screen light sits on the people. The rest of the room falls into deep shadow and disappears.

Read it as a still life that happens to contain humans — the objects are as important as the figures, and the absence between them is the subject.

02 · Mood & posture

Melancholic, cinematic, quietly observed.

Editorial book-cover aesthetic — think literary hardcover non-fiction, not thriller, not self-help. Painterly rather than journalistic; composed, not caught. The viewer should feel like they have walked into the room and paused at the threshold.

Emotional register: tender, not accusatory. The image critiques without sneering. There is love at this table, it is simply elsewhere.

03 · Do & don't

Do

  • Hold the top third mostly empty — title sits there.
  • Let shadow consume the edges; the scene is a lit island.
  • Keep phone screens slightly over-exposed — they are the only cold light source.
  • Treat untouched food as the emotional protagonist.
  • Favour a single, painterly exposure over HDR clarity.

Don't

  • No visible brand logos, app UIs, or readable screen content.
  • No hands reaching, no gestures of conflict — this is stillness.
  • No warm "golden hour" wash — the warmth is local to the dishes only.
  • No wide-angle distortion; keep perspective orthographic.
  • No text or watermarks baked into the image.

04 · Palette

Ink navy#0B1220
Deep navy#1A2744
Charcoal#1F2024
Amber#C98A2B
Bone#ECE4D3

Navy and charcoal dominate; amber appears only where firelight or a candle would fall (the food, never the faces). Bone reserved for paper surfaces — title, type, margin. A cold-warm duet, nothing in between.

Top third reserved for the title block. Image sits inside an 8% lateral margin. The table is centered within the frame; the three figures create a soft triangle, not a line. Horizon — in the sense of the table's far edge — should vanish into shadow, not crop hard.
